The menu,
stuck to the table

A sticker on every table. The customer taps their phone and your menu opens. No camera, no focusing, nothing to download. And when you raise a price you change it yourself from your phone: nothing needs reprinting, because nothing is printed.

NFC+QR
TABLE 7

The sticker carries the chip inside and the code printed on top: if a phone has no antenna, or has it switched off, the camera still works. Nobody is left out.

Four steps

01

Send me the menu however you have it

A sheet of paper, a photo, an old PDF. I put it into the system with its prices, its allergens and its sections.

02

You take the photos with your phone

In daylight, no filters. Nothing generated by a computer: Google rejects generated photos on business profiles, and since 2 August 2026 European law requires labelling them. A real photo avoids both, and it is what is actually on the plate.

03

I stick one on every table

Each one carries its number inside. When someone opens it from table 7, the WhatsApp button arrives already written: “I'm at table 7”. A photocopied code cannot do that.

04

From then on, you run it

You log in from your phone, change a price, and it changes on every table at once. Seasonal menus without throwing the old ones in the bin.

One menu,
two doors

What opens when the phone touches the table is your website. The same one that shows up on Google when somebody looks you up from home, and the same one you link from your social accounts. These are not two products: it is one, with a door in the customer's pocket and a door on the table.

That is why it costs less together than separately, and why the stickers are so cheap: the hard work is already done once you have the website.
