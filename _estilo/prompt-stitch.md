# Instrucciones para Stitch

Pegar tal cual al generar pantallas. Está en inglés porque Stitch responde
mejor así, y describe la DIRECCIÓN, no una obra concreta.

---

## Prompt base

```
Design a landing page in the style of 1980s Japanese airbrush illustration
(the lacquered, high-gloss look of city-pop record sleeves and mecha poster
art), adapted to a clean modern web layout.

VISUAL DIRECTION — follow all of these:

Colour: one saturated cobalt/ultramarine blue as a FLAT background field, no
gradient sky, no atmosphere. Chrome and silver rendered as hard-edged
gradient bands, not soft shading. Exactly one warm accent (vermilion orange
#FE4A23) used sparingly. Deep gold only in small details. Never two accents.

Light: a single hard light source from above. Cast shadows are solid black,
sharp-edged, never blurred. Add 4-point star sparkles at the brightest
highlights — this is the signature of the era.

Composition: low camera angle looking up, so the subject reads as monumental.
Subject cut cleanly against the flat colour field. Low or absent horizon.

Typography: extreme scale contrast. One enormous condensed sans-serif
headline in uppercase spanning the full width, set TONE ON TONE — same hue as
the background, roughly 12% lighter, never white. Around it, tiny 10px
monospace labels in columns like a technical data sheet, wide letter-spacing,
uppercase.

Surfaces: no outlines, no black keylines. Volume comes from light alone.
Matte flat areas against high-gloss chrome. Hard transitions between the two.

AVOID: soft pastel gradients, glow/bloom, drop shadows with blur, neon
cyberpunk purple-and-teal, glassmorphism, rounded friendly shapes, stock
3D renders.
```

## Para pantallas concretas, añadir al final

- **Portada** → `Hero section with a huge tone-on-tone headline, one product or
  figure shot from below, and a row of tiny technical labels underneath.`
- **Precios** → `Pricing laid out as a technical spec sheet: hairline rules,
  tabular numbers, tiny uppercase labels, no cards with rounded corners.`
- **Producto** → `Product presented as a monument: low angle, flat cobalt
  field behind it, hard black cast shadow, star sparkle on the chrome edge.`

## Lo que NO hay que pedirle

No pedir «al estilo de [nombre del artista]» ni subirle las tres láminas para
que las imite. Primero porque no sé de quién son, y segundo porque un
portfolio que dice «ninguna usa plantilla» no puede estar construido sobre la
obra de otro. Se describe la dirección; el resultado sale propio.
