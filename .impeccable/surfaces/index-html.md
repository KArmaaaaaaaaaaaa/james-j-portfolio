## Scope

The whole public site of James J Projects: home, pricing, menu-on-the-table,
Google profile, case study, dashboard demo. Six static pages, one generator.

Visitor mode: **Persuade**. The visitor decides and acts; the design is the
product, because what he sells IS design.

## Audience and job

A neighbourhood business owner, 40-65, not technical, who is losing customers
to the place next door that shows up properly on Google. They are not shopping
for a website; they are annoyed. They need to know what it costs and that this
person will not vanish.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A one-man studio whose work is proven by artefacts you can touch, not
adjectives. Refuses the agency landing page: no hero stock photo, no
three-benefit card row, no gradient blur.

OWN-WORLD: Japanese 80s airbrush over an Apple chassis. Flat cobalt fields
(#0f3fa8) alternating with paper white (#f2f2f0); chrome rendered as
hard-banded gradients that cut rather than blend; black cast shadows with zero
blur; four-point star sparkles at peak highlights; one warm accent (#fe4a23),
never two. Oswald condensed for display, Hanken Grotesk for prose, IBM Plex
Mono for the 10px spec labels. Sections read as plates in a book.

STORY: They understand each service in one line, believe it because the
artefact is live and touchable, and open the real bar site or write.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Paper ground. Kicker rule left. Three-line uppercase headline
at clamp(24px,8.6vw,118px) with the fuego rule drawn under the last phrase.
Signature line, availability pill, two square buttons flush against each other.
Primary action is the first button.

FORM: Pinned by the user across two sessions and recorded in memory; beats the
roll by contract. Candidate 6 of my grounded list was the assignment; seed key
e50792cc. RAISE — inversion (from the depot-blind challenger): rank is carried
by colour inversion between plates, never by growing type. RAISE — quantities
as interface (from the nixie challenger): prices are the largest object in
their region, with fixed digit positions.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
